Master Pre-Module Assessment
3.	Use the data WA_Fn-UseC_-Telco-Customer-Churn.csv to create a machine learning R script from scratch.  You will be expected to follow these steps in your R script:
a.	Sample – perform appropriate sampling of the data
b.	Explore – Explore the training data building visualizations, summary statistics and identify any issues in the data.
c.	Modify – Process the data to account for missing/NA, factors, and outliers.
d.	Model – Perform 3 modeling techniques and tune appropriate parameters, and/or change included variables.  The dataset is a “churn” prediction use case. 
e.	Assess – Assess each of the 3 modeling types to identify the best one.  You need to choose an appropriate metric like RMSE, MAPE, LogLoss, Tweedie Deviance etc based on your understanding of the problem.
